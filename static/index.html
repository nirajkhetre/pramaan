Procedure

How a hearing runs

  1. 01

    Summon

    Your question goes to four AI search engines at once through SerpApi: Google AI Mode, Google AI Overview, Bing Copilot and Brave AI Mode. A fifth search asks Google for official pages only (gov.in, nic.in, RBI, WHO).

  2. 02

    Take testimony

    From each answer Pramaan reads the figure that answers your question, with its unit, ranges like 70–99 mg/dL, lakh and crore, and “per 10 grams” versus “per gram”. It keeps the exact sentence and the latest date the answer mentions.

  3. 03

    Check the record

    Every cited page is classified as official, news, a company selling the product, a forum, or a page hijacked by spam. Official pages are read for figures and dates, so a stale page gives itself away.

  4. 04

    Stamp the verdict

    No AI model decides anything. Fixed, tested rules group the witnesses by agreement and set them against the record.
