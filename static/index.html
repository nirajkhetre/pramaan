Method

How a check runs

No AI model decides anything. Live search data goes in, and fixed, tested rules produce a verdict you can trace to a sentence and a link.

  1. 01

    Ask five ways

    The question goes to four AI answer engines and to one official-only Google search, all at once, through SerpApi.

    google_ai_modegoogle + google_ai_overviewbing_copilotbrave_ai_modegoogle · site:gov.in · nic.in · rbi.org.in · who.int
  2. 02

    Read the figure

    Each answer's key number is read with its unit (₹, lakh and crore, %, mg/dL, weeks), including ranges like 70–99 and "per 10 grams" versus "per gram". The exact sentence and the latest date it mentions are kept.

  3. 03

    Classify every source

    Every citation is tagged official, news, a company selling the product, a forum, reference, or a page hijacked with injected spam.

  4. 04

    Compare, with dates

    Engines are grouped by agreement, ranges included, then matched against statements on official pages. When the two conflict, dates decide which side is probably stale.

  5. 05

    Record everything

    Every response is saved to replay/ with the key removed, so any check replays with no key and no API calls.

Verdicts
